**Rejoinder v0.2.0: daily brief, Jira tab, Ask Jira, standups and insights**

Rejoinder watches your work chat, skips the noise and drafts replies in your own voice. You review and send; it never sends on its own.

**What's new**

- ✏️ **New name: Reply Agent is now Rejoinder.** Skills are `/rejoinder:setup`, `/rejoinder:drafts` and `/rejoinder:brief`; the old repo URL redirects here.
- 🌅 **Morning brief and day wrap** (`/rejoinder:brief`), pinned to the top of your Reply Queue
- 🎫 **Jira tab**: your open tickets, live, with filters, sorting and grouping
- 💬 **Ask Jira**: "how many tickets closed today?" or "bugs assigned to Sam", answered with status cards and a per-person breakdown
- ✍️ **Write my standup**: drafted in your voice from Jira and the replies you sent
- 🔀 **Jira quick actions**: move status or comment, each behind a confirm click
- 🔍 **PR review queue** and **Insights** (reply time, % sent as drafted, 7-day chart)
- 🌙 **Offline-safe hourly runs** using a cloud copy of your knowledge base
- 🔔 **Instant VIP alerts recipe** for Teams and Slack
- ✅ **CI checks** on every push
